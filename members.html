The Band

Members

01

Callum Reid

Lead Vocals / Guitar

The voice and vision. Callum founded Stellar Divide in 2017 after leaving art school, bringing a painter's instinct to the architecture of sound.

02

Noa Winters

Lead Guitar

Noa's playing defies easy categorisation — post-rock restraint one moment, howling feedback the next. She writes most of the guitar arrangements.

03

Dez Okafor

Bass / Backing Vocals

The anchor. Dez's bass lines are both melodic and muscular — the thread that holds the band's louder moments together.

04

Sian Park

Drums / Percussion

Sian drums like she's dismantling something. A background in jazz and electronic music gives her an approach that's precise and unpredictable in equal measure.
